## Release decision

v0.2.0 is a trust-first milestone that may validate one small product
hypothesis: a request-only Care Card helps a primary user communicate useful
care without exposing health context.

The implementation scope is exactly:

1. Gate 0 identity, authorization, relationship lifecycle, data rights,
   validation, and notification-idempotency work.
2. Gate 1 lean request-only Care Loop for one active partner.
3. The minimum domain-event and in-app inbox foundation needed to operate the
   pilot safely.

Care Loop is not a proven product wedge. Gate 1 cannot start a user pilot until
Gate 0 exits with tests and deployment evidence.

## Scope boundary

### Included

- canonical token identity and verified webhook ingress;
- locked roles, active-membership uniqueness, and secure invitations;
- symmetric unlink and safety reset;
- per-user chat hiding, data export, account deletion, and bounded lifecycle
  processing;
- consistent server-side period and pain date validation;
- idempotent scheduled notification effects and removal of direct sensitive
  Discord coupling;
- explicit Care Loop consent with immutable recipient snapshots;
- one active, expiring, request-only Care Card;
- one to three helpful actions and zero to three avoid actions;
- partner response of `acknowledged` or `cannot_help` only;
- owner revise, cancel, and revoke;
- immutable domain events, a recipient-safe in-app inbox, generic previews,
  dedupe, cancellation, and send-time authorization rechecks;
- authenticated two-role unit, integration, and E2E release coverage;
- an allowlisted pilot and server-side kill switches.

### Excluded

- health-detail sharing, health-derived summaries, outcomes, completion, chat,
  learned preferences, recaps, scores, streaks, or multiple active cards;
- confidence-aware cycle insights;
- native mobile, repository workspace migration, push launch, device-specific
  offline support, or biometric lock;
- HealthKit, Health Connect, health import, or health-platform write-back;
- provider reports, multi-partner support, AI coaching, community, quizzes, or
  games;
- a full multi-channel notification platform.

## Gate 0 workstreams

### G0-A: identity, roles, and invitations

Source: [spec 01](../specs/01-trust-boundaries-and-auth-identity.md).

Deliver:

- canonical `identity.tokenIdentifier` lookup with a measured legacy migration;
- exact raw-body Clerk verification and internal, idempotent profile sync;
- immediate disablement for deleted provider users;
- one-time role assignment and audited recovery only;
- one active membership per user and one authoritative primary per active
  couple;
- high-entropy, digest-only, short-lived, single-use invitations with durable
  attempt controls.

Exit when issues #1, #2, #3, and #6 are closed and fresh-sign-in,
issuer-collision, replay, role, duplicate-membership, and invitation race tests
pass in the target environments.

### G0-B: lifecycle, data rights, and safety reset

Source: [spec 02](../specs/02-relationship-lifecycle-data-rights-and-safety-reset.md).

Deliver:

- symmetric normal unlink and safety reset;
- immediate revocation across reads, writes, presence, invitations, assisted
  logging, and pending delivery;
- terminal relationship identities; relink creates a new couple;
- per-user chat hiding and policy-controlled sender deletion;
- classified export, erasure, retention, and bounded resumable lifecycle jobs;
- privacy-safe audit with no safety reason exposed to the counterpart.

Exit when issue #5 is closed and concurrency tests prove no shared write or
delivery can commit after relationship closure. Export and erasure reconciliation
must report zero unapproved residual records.

### G0-C: date validation and integrity

Deliver one server-side calendar-date contract for period and pain mutations.
Reject malformed, impossible, unauthorized future, and inconsistent ranges;
cover timezone/calendar boundaries without trusting client dates for authority.

Exit when issues #7 and #10 are closed with unit/integration evidence and
existing timeline/correction flows still pass.

### G0-D: notification idempotency and containment

Source: [spec 05](../specs/05-care-event-outbox-inbox-and-notifications.md).

Deliver stable identities for current scheduled facts and external attempts,
bounded retry behavior, generic previews, and a send-time authorization recheck.
Direct domain-to-Discord calls must not remain a path for Care Loop or sensitive
payloads. Discord is an isolated legacy adapter only.

Exit when issue #9 closes and repeated schedules, concurrent workers,
pre-accept failures, ambiguous outcomes, revocation races, and missing adapter
configuration create no duplicate or unauthorized effect.

### G0-E: trust regression gate

Make the release-critical auth fixture reliable in CI. Cover primary and partner
sign-in, onboarding, link, consent settings, assisted period logging, timeline,
message visibility, unlink, safety reset, account disablement, and denial after
closure. Keep unit, typecheck, build, and authenticated E2E green.

### Gate 0 exit criteria

Gate 0 exits only when:

- all G0-A through G0-E criteria pass on the target commit;
- high-severity trust issues are closed or explicitly risk-accepted by Product,
  Engineering, and Privacy/Security owners;
- migrations/backfills are measured, dry-run, bounded, and reversible;
- logs can reconstruct authorization and lifecycle decisions without content;
- revocation and safety reset synchronously stop access and pending disclosure;
- no unilateral shared-history destruction remains;
- a production-like two-account smoke test passes.

## Gate 1 workstreams

### G1-A: consent and recipient snapshot

Source: [spec 03](../specs/03-consent-sharing-and-care-loop-privacy.md).

Deliver a disabled-by-default Care Loop pilot permission, explicit owner
publication, `request_only` as the only mode, recipient/couple-bound immutable
snapshots, expiry, cancellation, revocation, non-inference, and privacy-safe
audit. Existing phase, pain, and assisted-write flags do not authorize Care
Loop and Care Loop does not broaden them.

Exit when the effective-consent truth table, exact partner DTO allowlist,
cross-couple denial, stale-cache revocation, and notification-consent race tests
pass.

### G1-B: request domain and state machine

Source: [spec 04](../specs/04-care-loop-v1-domain-and-state-machine.md).

Deliver:

- one active owner request with immutable revisions;
- one to three curated helpful actions and zero to three avoid actions;
- bounded custom variants only;
- partner response of `acknowledged` or `cannot_help`, once per revision;
- owner revise, cancel, and revoke;
- trusted-server expiry, scheduled materialization, and bounded reconciliation;
- required client mutation IDs, expected revisions, and idempotent receipts.

Exit when every allowed/rejected transition, duplicate, stale revision, exact
expiry boundary, scheduler delay, and unlink/revoke race passes. No health field,
completion state, outcome, chat, learning, or external side effect may appear.

### G1-C: event and in-app inbox foundation

Source: [spec 05](../specs/05-care-event-outbox-inbox-and-notifications.md).

Deliver immutable deduplicated events transactionally with Care Loop changes;
one recipient-safe, paginated inbox item per event; unread/read/archive state;
cancellation by entity/relationship scope; privacy-safe metrics and operations.
Care Loop external delivery remains off during the pilot. The pipeline may
shadow-plan generic delivery, but no push/email/SMS or Care Loop Discord alert
is in scope.

Exit when repeated mutations/planners create one event and inbox item, inbox
reads leak no event/private document, and cancel/expire/unlink/revoke invalidates
access without unbounded scans.

### G1-D: UI, accessibility, and E2E

Deliver an owner composer and confirmation showing recipient, exact shared
actions, and expiry; an active-card owner view; a partner card with only the two
responses; and privacy-safe loading, empty, retry, expired, cancelled, revoked,
disabled, and offline states.

Meet keyboard, screen-reader, focus, reduced-motion, non-color, responsive, and
44-by-44-pixel target requirements. E2E must cover create in under 30 seconds,
real-time partner read/response, revise, cancel, revoke, expiry, and relationship
closure while the card is open.

### Gate 1 exit criteria

- Gate 0 remains green on the exact candidate commit.
- All G1-A through G1-D acceptance and privacy tests pass.
- Server kill switches block publication, partner reads/actions, and event/inbox
  planning without destructive rollback.
- Synthetic two-account validation covers happy, denied, duplicate, stale,
  expired, cancelled, revoked, and relationship-race paths.
- Support has privacy incident and pilot-disable runbooks.
- Product, Engineering, Privacy/Security, and Design approve invited pilot entry.

## Pilot phases

### Phase 0: synthetic/internal

- Enable only team-owned accounts through a server allowlist.
- Exercise every failure, race, rollback, accessibility, and redaction path.
- Verify stored DTOs/events/telemetry, not only rendered UI.

### Phase 1: first invited cohort

- Enable no more than five couples.
- Review every unwanted-sharing, coercion, scope-confusion, and unavailable-card
  report before expansion.
- Keep request-only sharing and the two-response model fixed unless safety
  requires a correction.

### Phase 2: evidence cohort

- Expand incrementally toward at least 15 activated couples and 50 published
  cards.
- Target median creation at or below 30 seconds, repeat creation by at least 40%
  of activated couples, response before expiry on at least 60% of unrevoked
  cards, comprehension by at least 70% of surveyed primary users, and intended
  boundary representation by at least 80%.
- Treat cancellation, `cannot_help`, and expiry as valid outcomes, not partner
  failure.

### Phase 3: decision

Exclude synthetic activity and review metrics, interviews, privacy reports, and
operational burden together. Explicitly choose one:

- graduate the request-only Care Loop;
- revise and run another bounded pilot;
- stop Care Loop and retain only the trust foundation.

No metric automatically authorizes a broader sharing mode or deferred feature.

## Rollback triggers

Immediately stop the pilot for:

- confirmed cross-user, cross-couple, or post-revocation disclosure;
- private health, custom request, private note, or safety content in an
  unauthorized DTO, event, log, analytics payload, or external preview;
- cancellation, expiry, unlink, safety reset, or deletion failing to block
  reads/actions;
- an identity/authorization regression affecting existing product data;
- duplicate or unauthorized external provider effects;
- repeated coercion, privacy-pressure, or scope-confusion reports.

Pause cohort expansion when more than 5% of cards require correction because
displayed scope differed from intent, more than 25% of enabled users opt out for
privacy/pressure reasons, or operational failure prevents reliable expiry and
revocation monitoring.

Rollback behavior:

1. Disable new Care Loop create/revise operations server-side.
2. Deny partner Care Card reads and responses.
3. Suppress new event/inbox planning and cancel unsent deliveries.
4. Keep owner cancel/revoke, read-time expiry, lifecycle closure, and bounded
   reconciliation operational.
5. Preserve owner-private data and audit evidence under approved retention.
6. Leave unrelated period, pain, timeline, and messaging available unless they
   share the incident boundary.
7. Never reactivate a terminal request/relationship or restore an older
   snapshot.

Rollback does not require a destructive schema reversal.
